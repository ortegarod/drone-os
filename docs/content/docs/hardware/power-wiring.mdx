# Power and Wiring

Power and wiring are where otherwise-correct software integrations can still fail in practice.

## Why this deserves its own page

Companion computers, cameras, modems, and flight controllers all compete for:

- available power budget
- stable voltage delivery
- clean wiring and connector layout
- mechanical reliability in the airframe

## Documentation direction

This section should evolve to document:

- companion-computer power requirements
- modem and camera power implications
- USB and serial wiring paths
- enclosure and mounting constraints
- practical integration notes learned from real-hardware builds

## Practical rule

As new Jetson and cellular integrations are added, power and wiring notes should be documented alongside them instead of being buried inside architecture docs.
