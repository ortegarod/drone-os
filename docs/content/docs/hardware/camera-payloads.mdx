# Camera and Payloads

Cameras and payload devices sit on top of the core flight stack and companion-computer foundation.

## Typical roles

Payload-side hardware may include:

- RGB cameras
- mission-specific sensors
- perception sensors
- future onboard inference inputs

## Why this matters

Payload hardware affects:

- compute requirements
- power draw
- mounting and enclosure constraints
- network bandwidth and storage needs

## Relationship to companion computer choice

Payload complexity is one of the reasons to support more than one companion-computer path.

A basic camera workflow may be fine on a lighter compute budget.

A heavier perception workflow is one of the reasons the Jetson path is being added.
